A smooth appointment starts here

Come prepared

A little preparation helps me give your hair the attention it deserves.

Before you arrive

  • Please come with a clean scalp.
  • Hair should be free of product buildup and heavy oils.
  • Come with your hair detangled and ready for your service, unless we have discussed otherwise.

Respect the time

  • Please arrive on time for your appointment.
  • Make sure you have selected the correct service and part size when sending your request.
  • If you have inspiration photos, have them ready to share.

Booking policies

Location · GSU based

If you live on the GSU campus, I come to you. If you live off campus, you come to me. We’ll confirm the location details when your appointment is arranged.

Late arrival

Please plan ahead and communicate if you are running behind. If you are 30 minutes late, your appointment is automatically cancelled.

Availability

Appointments are available Thursday through Sunday. Booking requests are subject to confirmation.

Respect the process

Please choose your service carefully, arrive with a clean scalp and no product, and communicate any changes before your appointment.
